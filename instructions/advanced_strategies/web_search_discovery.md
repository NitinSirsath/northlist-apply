# Discovery and job sources

Adds only these steps to `AGENTS.md`; if the two ever conflict, `AGENTS.md` wins.

## Discovery (web search)
1. Search the web with the target job titles, locations and work modes from `personal_data/profile.md`. No saved career URLs are needed.
2. Prefer direct application pages, such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday. Avoid job boards that only lead back to LinkedIn or Indeed.
3. When a site shows freshness controls, use them (`AGENTS.md` section 9); if not, say so instead of calling the results fresh.

## Job sources
These plan items only find jobs. Each job is applied to where it is actually submitted.
- **The Reliable Jobs** (`https://thereliablejobs.com/`): find the job, open its application link, and continue with the matching steps below.
- **TEKsystems** (`https://www.teksystems.com/en/careers`): company_direct steps from the careers page; a Workday form follows the Workday steps.
- **Teksands** (`https://teksands.ai/`): company_direct steps for its current Teksands or Hire4X job form; treat it as a custom system and fill only what the page shows.
- **D4hire** (`https://d4hire.in/`): a recruitment agency site. If it shows no specific job with an application route, report the source as unavailable.
- **Supersourcing** (`https://supersourcing.com/`): company_direct steps for one specific job's form. A profile or talent sign-up alone is not an application.
- **We Work Remotely** (`https://weworkremotely.com/`): not supported yet. Its **Apply for this position** link needs a job-seeker account, and the sign-up page has a security check. Never use it as a plan item and never open its jobs. When search finds a job there, look for the same job on the company's own career page and apply there, or leave it.

## Every found job
1. Open the job and look at its real application page.
2. Fill `Selected plan choice` (the plan item), `Discovery source` (the job source or website) and `Actual application destination` (where the form is sent).
3. Continue this exact job where it is, never with a new search:
   - a Workday form: `instructions/platforms/workday_strategy.md`;
   - Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or another company form: `instructions/advanced_strategies/company_direct_apply.md`;
   - anything else: use the closest of these steps without inventing anything, or report the destination as unavailable.
